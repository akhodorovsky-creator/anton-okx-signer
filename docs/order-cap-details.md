The guarded launcher enforces a per-buy ceiling of 20 EUR while preserving a total bot exposure cap of 200 EUR. Exchange minSz is checked against intended order value; the bot must skip a buy rather than increase size beyond the configured cap. If existing holdings become unsellable because of minSz or available balance, logging that condition does not mean the position is closed. The order history endpoints are time-limited; dashboard P&L is an estimate.
